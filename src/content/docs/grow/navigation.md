Every page of your site has a menu at the top, a footer at the bottom and a breadcrumb trail ("Home › Programs › Infants") so families always know where they are and how to get around. **Grow → Navigation** controls how they look. You can also get there from the **Navigation** button above the preview in the page editor.

Colors and fonts come from your [theme](/grow/theme/), so the menu always matches the rest of the site. A live preview shows your choices on any of your pages, on a computer or a phone, before you save.

## Layout

Choose where your page links go:

- **Top menu**: links in a bar across the top of each page.
- **Footer**: no top bar. Links sit in the footer.
- **Both**: links in the top menu and repeated in the footer. This is the default.

With a top menu you also choose:

- **Menu style**: **Classic** (your name on the left, links on the right), **Centered** (your name centered with the links in a row beneath it) or **Minimal** (just your name and a menu button that opens the links).
- **Menu color**: **White**, **Soft** (your theme's soft tint), **Brand** (your primary color with white text) or **Dark**.
- **Stay at the top while scrolling**: keeps the menu visible on long pages.

On phones, every menu style folds its links behind a menu button.

## Name and logo

The left of the menu shows your logo, your center's name, or both. Upload a logo or pick one from your media library. The name defaults to your center name from settings; type a different one to override it. Clicking the name or logo takes families to your home page.

## Links

With **Automatic** on, the menu lists every top-level page, starting with home. Pages you add later appear on their own. Pages under another page (like `/programs/infants` under `/programs`) are left out so the menu stays short.

Turn **Automatic** off to choose the links yourself: rename them, reorder them, remove pages you don't want in the menu, or link to a web address, an email address or a phone number. You can have up to 12 links.

**Highlighted button** adds a button at the end of the menu, such as "Schedule a tour", styled like the other buttons on your site.

:::tip[Keep the menu short]
Five or six links is plenty. Families are looking for tuition, programs, and how to book a tour. A long menu makes all three harder to find.
:::

## Footer

- **Simple**: one centered row of links.
- **Columns**: your name and address, your links, and your phone, email and hours from your center settings.

The footer has the same four colors as the menu. It always ends with a copyright line and "Powered by Kinderly Grow".

## Breadcrumbs

Breadcrumbs show the path to the current page, like **Home › Programs › Infant room**. They appear on every page except your home page. You can show them **below the menu** or **above the footer**, or hide them. You can also choose the separator between steps (›, / or ·).

Breadcrumbs also help search engines like Google show your site's structure in search results.

## Saving

**Save navigation** puts your changes live right away. Unlike page edits, there's nothing to publish. If you link to a page you haven't published yet, families get a "page not found" until you [publish](/grow/sites/#drafts-and-publishing) it.
