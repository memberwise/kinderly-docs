**Grow → Settings** is everything about your site that isn't its content: where families find it, the daily digest email, and what Grow includes on your plan.

## Site address

Every site gets a free address at `yourname.kinderlysites.com`. **Change** picks a different subdomain: 2–32 characters, lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens.

:::caution[The old address stops working straight away]
Changing the subdomain retires the old one immediately. Anyone with the old link sees a "site not found" page, so update the links you've shared (your Google Business profile, social pages, flyers) at the same time.
:::

The card also shows whether your site is published. Until you publish from [Pages](/grow/sites/), families can't see it at either address.

## Custom domain

On Bloom and above you can use a domain you own, like `www.yourcenter.com`:

1. Enter the domain and choose **Connect**. Use a subdomain such as `www`.
2. Kinderly shows the DNS records to add at your domain provider: a **CNAME**, and usually a **TXT** record for the security certificate. Each has a **Copy** button.
3. Add them at your provider. They can take a few minutes, sometimes up to an hour, to take effect. The card shows *Waiting for DNS…*, then *Issuing certificate…*, then **Live**. You can check again at any time.

Once it's live, your site loads at your domain with a secure (https) connection. Your `kinderlysites.com` address keeps working too.

## Daily digest email

Turn on **Send the daily digest** and each morning everyone on your team gets an email with yesterday's site visits, top pages, button clicks and where visitors came from. Days with no visits are skipped. It's off until you turn it on, and it's included on Bloom and above.

## Your plan

The last card lists what Grow includes on your plan (website pages, virtual tours, pipeline boards, analytics, the daily digest and a custom domain) and how much of each you're using. Grow is part of every Kinderly plan. See [What you get](/getting-started/what-you-get/) for how the plans differ.
