Grant funding is a real income stream for childcare providers, and it's usually missed for two reasons: not knowing an opportunity existed until after the deadline, and not being able to lay hands on a current W-9 the week it's due.

The Funding Center is built for both problems.

## What it does

- **[Opportunities](/funding/opportunities/)** — grants matched to your center, not a general list you have to sift.
- **[Applications](/funding/applications/)** — track what you're going for, with each one's deadline, document checklist and reporting requirements together.
- **[Calendar](/funding/calendar/)** — deadlines, reporting dates and document expiries in one view.
- **[Documents](/funding/documents/)** — upload the standard paperwork once; it attaches to every application that asks.
- **[Profile](/funding/profile/)** — the answers that decide what you match.

## Start with the profile

Matching is only as good as what Kinderly knows about you. An incomplete profile means a thin, generic list.

The profile takes a few minutes and you fill it in once. See [Funding profile](/funding/profile/).

:::tip[Do the profile before you go looking]
Browsing opportunities with an empty profile is the main reason people conclude "there's nothing here". Most grants are restricted by state, licence status, provider type or quality rating — Kinderly can't match on what it hasn't been told.
:::

## The workflow

1. Complete your **profile**.
2. Browse **opportunities** matched to you.
3. **Track** the ones worth going for — they become applications.
4. Upload the **documents** each one asks for.
5. Work to the **calendar**.

## Plan availability

The Funding Center isn't on the free tier. Bloom tracks 5 grants; Flourish and Grove are unlimited. See [What you get](/getting-started/what-you-get/).

:::tip[One grant usually pays for the year]
If you're weighing the subscription, this is the part that tends to settle it. A single modest equipment or quality-improvement grant caught before its deadline generally covers the annual cost several times over.
:::
