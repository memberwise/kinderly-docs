You can sign in to Kinderly with an email and password, or with **Google** or
**Microsoft**. They all reach the same account.

## Using Google or Microsoft for the first time

If you already have a Kinderly account and then sign in with Google using the
same email address, Kinderly connects the two. You'll be asked to sign in once
more to finish — that's expected, and it only happens the one time. After that,
either button works and you land in the same place.

Nothing moves or changes when this happens. Same center, same children, same
history, same colleagues.

:::tip[Use the same email address]
The connection is made on your email address. Signing in with a Google account
on a *different* address creates a separate Kinderly account, with its own empty
center — which is almost never what anyone wants.
:::

## When Kinderly won't connect them

Occasionally you'll see a message saying we couldn't confirm the accounts belong
to the same person. That's deliberate, and it's protecting you: connecting sign-in
methods on an unverified email address is how someone else could get into your
center.

You'll see it when:

- The email address on the new sign-in method hasn't been confirmed.
- The Kinderly account on that address was never confirmed either.
- More than one Kinderly account uses that address.

In every case, sign in the way you did before — you'll get straight in — and
[get in touch](mailto:support@mykinderly.com) and we'll connect them for you.

## Staff signing in to the apps

Staff use the same email and password for the [Teachers app](/mobile/teacher-app/)
as they do on the web, and guardians the same for the
[Parents app](/mobile/parent-app/). Adding a Google or Microsoft sign-in on the
web doesn't change what they use on their phone.
