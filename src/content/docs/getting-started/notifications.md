The **bell** at the bottom of the left sidebar, just above Settings, is where Kinderly tells you about things that need your attention: a family's payment, a classroom out of ratio, a food program claim that hasn't been filed. It's on every page, and you don't need to set anything up.

## Knowing something came in

- A **red number** on the bell shows how many notifications you haven't read yet (9+ when there are more than nine).
- When something new arrives, the bell **rings**, and a **pop-up** appears in the corner with a **View** button that takes you straight to it.
- The **browser tab** shows the count too, as "(2) Kinderly", so you can see it from another tab.

The bell checks for new notifications every 30 seconds, and again as soon as you come back to the tab.

## Reading and clearing

Click the bell to open the panel. Unread notifications are in bold with a dot beside them, and you can switch between **All** and **Unread**.

- **Click a notification** to mark it read and open the page it's about: the family's billing account, the classroom, the Food Program tab.
- **Click the ×** to dismiss it. It's gone from your bell but not from your co-workers'.
- **Mark all read** clears the red number but keeps everything in the list.
- **Clear all** empties the list.

Some notifications are about a situation that's still going on, like a classroom that's out of ratio or an integration that keeps failing. These are kept as **one notification that updates itself** rather than a new one every few minutes. When the problem fixes itself (the room is back in ratio, or a staff member replies to the parent), the notification is marked **Resolved** and no longer counts toward the red number. If a problem drags on, the notification comes back as unread now and then, so it can't get lost.

Notifications stay in the bell for 60 days.

## What you'll be notified about

| Notification | When |
|---|---|
| **Payments received** | A family pays an invoice in the parent app, or a bank payment clears. Autopay is grouped into one running total per day rather than one notification per family. |
| **Failed payments & refunds** | A card is declined, a bank debit bounces, an autopay charge fails, or a refund you issued doesn't go through. The invoice is open again, so you'll want to follow up. |
| **Out-of-ratio classrooms** | A room has too few staff clocked in for the children who are signed in. Updates as the headcount changes and resolves itself when the room is back in ratio. |
| **Upcoming classroom moves** | A planned move, or a child aging into the next room, comes within your classroom-move lead time. Each move appears once, and resolves after its date passes or it's completed or cancelled. |
| **Food program (CACFP)** | A meal count hasn't been started, a finalized count has errors, last month's claim hasn't been filed, or eligibility forms are expiring. See [Food Program](/manage/food-program/). |
| **Expiring documents** | A document, person file, shared link or staff certificate is close to its expiration date. |
| **Integration problems** | A scheduled KinderConnect submission failed, or attendance hasn't reached the state for longer than your schedule allows. |
| **Parent messages** | A guardian replies to a conversation in the parent app. A busy conversation stays as one notification showing the latest message. |
| **Forms submitted** | Someone completes a form you shared with them. |
| **New leads** | A new enquiry reaches your pipeline from a form or your website. Leads you add by hand aren't announced. |

Payments you record yourself, such as cash, a check, or account credit, don't create a notification, because you already know about them.

## Choosing what shows up

Go to **Settings → Notifications**, or click the gear at the top of the bell's panel, and turn any of the categories above on or off. Your choices are **just for you**: turning off payment notifications doesn't turn them off for a co-director. If you turn a category back on, notifications from the past 60 days in that category reappear.

Two settings that affect the whole center live elsewhere, under **Manage → Settings → App settings**:

- **Out-of-ratio alerts** only run while ratio alerts are turned on for the center.
- The **lead time** for classroom moves (how many days ahead you're told) is set there too.

The bell is separate from the push notifications staff get in the Teachers app and from Kinderly's emails. Turning a category off here doesn't change either of those.
