**Print sheets** turns a record into a simple page you can pin up: a child's photo, classroom, who can pick them up and their allergies, for example. You pick what goes on it, save the layout for your team, and print one sheet or a hundred.

Sheets are built from your records **at the moment you print**, so they're never out of date. Reprint after a change instead of editing paper.

## Printing

- **One person:** open a child, guardian or staff member and click **Print Sheet** next to the other quick actions.
- **Several at once:** on **Children**, **Guardians & Contacts** or **Staff**, tick the people you want and click **Print Sheets** in the bar at the bottom. Up to 200 at a time.

Click **Print** and your browser's print dialog opens. Choose a printer, or **Save as PDF** to keep or email a copy.

:::tip[A sheet for every child in a classroom]
Use [Filters](/manage/children/#the-list) to show one classroom, tick the box at the top of the list to select everyone, then **Print Sheets**.
:::

## Choosing what's on the sheet

The dialog lists every field you can print, grouped by section. Tick what you want, then drag the fields in **Print order** to arrange them.

| Kind | What you can print |
|---|---|
| Child | Name, birth date and age, gender, classroom, programs, address; primary contact, parents and guardians, emergency contacts, everyone authorized to pick up, siblings; allergies, physician, preferred hospital, insurance, last physical, emergency treatment consent, immunizations; custom fields |
| Guardian | Name, phones, email, address; their children (with relationship and classroom); employer; custom fields |
| Staff | Name, title, role, classroom, hire date, birth date; phones, email, address; certifications and when they expire; custom fields |

Two layouts:

- **One per page:** a full sheet per person, large and easy to read from across a room.
- **Compact cards, several per page:** for a classroom roster or a binder.

**Include photo** adds the person's picture where they have one.

:::note[Some things are never printable]
Social Security numbers, pay and billing balances aren't offered at all, so no layout can put them on paper. Hidden custom fields stay off too.
:::

## Saving layouts

Give a layout a name, such as *Classroom door sheet* or *Allergy card*, and **Save layout**. Saved layouts are shared with your whole team and appear in the **Layout** menu the next time anyone prints.

A layout belongs to one kind of record: a child layout only appears when printing children, and can't be used for guardians or staff.
