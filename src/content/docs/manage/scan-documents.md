**Scan a document** turns a stack of paper into Kinderly records. Photograph a form, and Ellie reads it, handwriting included. She works out where each answer belongs and hands you a filled-in record to check. Retyping a family's enrollment packet takes about an hour; checking a scan takes a couple of minutes.

Nothing is saved until you've reviewed it and pressed **Apply**.

:::note[Bloom and above, administrators only]
Scanning is part of the **Bloom plan and above**. On the free plan, **Scan Document** explains the upgrade instead; a scan already read can still be reviewed and applied after a downgrade.

It's available to Manage users on the web and to staff with the **Administrator** role in the [Kinderly Staff app](/mobile/teacher-app/). It's part of Ellie, so it uses your Ellie allowance like everything else Ellie does.
:::

## Starting a scan

Where you start tells Ellie what you're adding:

| Start from | Ellie looks for | You can apply once |
|---|---|---|
| **Children → Add Child ▾ → Scan Document** | The child first, then their parents and contacts, allergies, doctor, insurance and immunizations | the child has a first and last name |
| **Guardians & Contacts → Add Guardian ▾ → Scan Document** | Every parent, guardian and emergency contact on the form. The child is optional | each new adult has a first and last name |
| **Staff → Add Staff ▾ → Scan Document** | One staff member: name, email, phones, title, birth date and start date | the staff member has a name and an email address |
| A child's page → **Scan Document** | The same as from Children, matched to that child | always |

Any paper form works, with no templates or setup: state forms like Missouri's 3317, your own enrollment packet, a doctor's note, an allergy action plan, an immunization card, a staff application.

## Photographing the pages

Each document can have up to six pages. Lay the page flat, in good light, and fill the frame. A phone photo is fine.

To scan several documents in one go, use **Add another document** and give each one its own pages. **Read N documents** uploads them all and queues them.

## You don't have to wait

Ellie usually takes 20 to 60 seconds per document, and you don't have to watch:

- A notification in the corner, **Ellie is reading 2 documents…**, follows you anywhere in Manage.
- As each one finishes, a **Ready to review** notification appears with the name of the child or person it's about. Click **Review** to go straight to it.
- The **Recent scans** list on the scan page shows every recent scan and where it's up to: *Waiting*, *Reading*, *Ready to review*, *Couldn't read* or *Saved*.

:::caution[Keep Kinderly open in your browser]
You can move around Kinderly freely while scans are read, but closing the tab pauses them. They pick up again the next time you open Kinderly.
:::

## Reviewing a scan

The photo of the paper sits next to what Ellie filled in, one step at a time. A child scan has five steps: **Child**, **Adults**, **Health**, **Everything else** and **Confirm**. Scans from Guardians start with **Adults**, and staff scans have just **Staff member** and **Confirm**.

### Messy handwriting

Ellie gives her best guess and tells you when she isn't sure:

- An **amber** field means *check this*. Other likely readings sit underneath, one click away ("Or: 3/9/22").
- A **red** field means Ellie couldn't read it at all. Type it from the photo.
- Under each field, the label it was read from ("Child's date of birth, page 1") jumps the photo to that page.
- **Next to check** jumps to the next amber field. Editing a field, or ticking **Looks right**, clears it.

Ellie also checks her readings against what you already have. A date has to be a real date, a phone number needs ten digits, and a name that matches someone on file is treated as confirmed.

### People you already have

Ellie looks for existing records before creating new ones:

- A **child** with the same name and birth date is offered as *Update Mia Stone*, and the review shows what's on file next to what was read.
- An **adult** with the same phone number or email is linked rather than added again. A matching name alone is offered as a choice.
- A **staff member** with the same email is offered as *Update*. Only blank fields on their record are filled in; nothing on file is replaced.

### Things Kinderly has no field for

Answers that don't belong anywhere, such as "Favorite song", are listed under **Couldn't be placed**. Kept ones are saved together as a note on the child, or on the adults if there's no child. **Ignore all** drops them in one go.

## What Apply does

| Scan | Apply saves |
|---|---|
| Child | The child (new or updated); each adult, linked with their relationship; the address; allergies; medical details; immunizations; leftovers as one note |
| Guardians | Each adult, and the child too if the form names one |
| Staff | A new staff member, who is emailed a sign-in for the [Kinderly Staff app](/mobile/teacher-app/) just as with **Add Staff**, or the blanks filled in on an existing one |

Adults are added by the same rules as adding them by hand: everyone **Can Pick Up** unless the form says otherwise, and a child always has exactly one **Primary** contact. See [Family & Contacts](/manage/children/#family--contacts).

The photos are filed under **Documents** on the child and on every adult from the scan, or on the staff member, so the signed paper is always one click away.

:::tip[Recycle the stack]
Once a scan is applied, the original pages are in Kinderly. You don't need the paper copy in a filing cabinet as well.
:::

## Privacy

Scans hold children's medical details and parents' personal information, so Kinderly keeps no spare copies:

- The working copy of the photos is deleted **the moment you apply** a scan. The only copy left is the one filed with the family or staff member.
- **Discard scan** deletes the photos immediately.
- A scan that's started and never finished is deleted after **7 days**.

## Updating an existing guardian or staff member

On a guardian's or staff member's page, **Scan Document** reads a form for that person. A short list shows each field where the form differs from what's on file, side by side. Blank fields are ticked for you; replacing something already on file is up to you.

## In the staff app

The [Kinderly Staff app](/mobile/teacher-app/#scanning-paper-forms) scans the same way, with the same choice of what you're adding, several documents at once, and reading in the background.
