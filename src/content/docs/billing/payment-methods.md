**Settings → Payment & Billing**.

## Cards

**Add Card** saves a payment method. When you have more than one, a **Default** badge marks the one that gets charged. The bin icon removes a card.

:::caution[Keep the default card current]
A card that expires means a failed renewal, and a failed renewal eventually drops you to the free tier — with a 10-child cap and 30 MB of storage — potentially mid-term. Check the expiry on your default card once a year; it's the least dramatic five minutes of admin you'll do.
:::

:::tip[Use a center card, not a personal one]
Subscriptions on someone's personal card become a problem when that person is on leave or leaves. A center account or a shared finance card keeps the service attached to the organisation.
:::

## Invoices

**View Invoices** shows your billing history and lets you download past invoices — for your own [Finance](/manage/finance/) records or your accountant.

:::tip[Log the subscription as an expense]
Kinderly is a business cost. Downloading the invoice and recording it in [Finance](/manage/finance/) under a software or admin category means it's already there at year end rather than something you reconstruct from bank statements.
:::

## Usage

**Usage** shows consumption against your plan's limits — the same figures as the dashboard bars. Worth a look before changing tier.

## Not the same as family billing

This page is what *you* pay Kinderly. What *families* pay you is [Manage → Billing](/manage/billing/), which has its own payment setup, accounts and invoices.
