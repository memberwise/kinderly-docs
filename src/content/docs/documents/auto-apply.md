Some documents belong to everyone: the family handbook, your sick policy, the staff handbook, an infant feeding plan. **Auto-apply documents** lets you choose a document from your library once and decide who gets it. Everyone you choose has it straight away, and so does every family, child or staff member you add from then on. You don't have to attach it to each person by hand.

:::note[Bloom and above]
Auto-apply documents is part of the **Bloom plan and above**. On Sprout, the page explains the upgrade instead. If you move down to Sprout, your auto-apply documents are kept but stop showing until you upgrade again.
:::

## Creating one

Open **Enroll → Auto-Apply** and press **New auto-apply document**. A short wizard walks you through three steps:

1. **Document**: pick a file from your [Documents library](/documents/overview/). Search by name or folder.
2. **Who gets it**: for **Guardians**, **Children** and **Staff**, choose one of:
   - **No one**
   - **Everyone**: everyone you have now and everyone you add later
   - **Choose**: tick specific people. Search, or use **Select all** on what's showing.

   You can mix these. For example, give the handbook to all guardians, and an infant feeding plan to three children.
3. **Review**: set the name families and staff will see (it starts as the file name), add an optional note such as "Please read before your child's first day", and press **Create**.

**Shortcut:** in Documents, open a file's **⋮** menu and choose **Auto-apply to people…**. The wizard opens with that file already picked.

## Who sees what

| Applied to | Where it shows |
|---|---|
| **Guardians** | In the [Parents app](/mobile/parent-app/) under **My Documents**, and in the **Auto-Applied Documents** box on the guardian's **Documents** tab |
| **Children** | On the child's **Documents** tab, and to that child's families in the Parents app under the child's name |
| **Staff** | In the **Auto-Applied Documents** box on the staff member's **Documents** tab |

In the Parents app, these documents are marked **From your center**. Families can open them but can't delete them.

## Editing, pausing and deleting

Each auto-apply document is a card on the **Auto-Apply** page. The card shows who it reaches, for example *All guardians · 42* or *3 children*.

- **Edit**: click the card, or **⋮ → Edit**. You can change the document, who gets it, the name or the note. Changes apply to everyone straight away.
- **Pause**: turn off the switch on the card. The document disappears for everyone until you turn it back on, and your settings are kept.
- **Delete**: **⋮ → Delete**. Nobody receives it automatically any more. The file stays in your Documents library.

## When the file changes

- **Renaming or moving** the file in Documents is fine. The auto-apply document follows it.
- **Replacing** the file's contents means everyone sees the new version the next time they open it. This is the easiest way to roll out a revised handbook.
- **Deleting** the file from Documents also deletes any auto-apply documents that use it. The delete dialog warns you first and names them.

:::tip[Auto-apply or share?]
Auto-apply is for documents people **read**. If you need a family to **fill in or sign** something and send it back, [send it as a share](/shares/sending/) instead.
:::
