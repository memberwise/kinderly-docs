## Staff (web)

Staff sign in to the Kinderly dashboard with their own account. Each staff member should have their own login rather than sharing one — it's what makes the audit trail meaningful and lets you remove access cleanly when someone leaves.

Logins are created by inviting people to your [team](/getting-started/account-setup/#your-team).

## Staff and families (mobile)

The [Teacher](/mobile/teacher-app/) and [Parent](/mobile/parent-app/) apps use the same account system as the web dashboard.

Families get access through the email address on their [guardian record](/manage/guardians/) — which is why a typo there quietly cuts a parent off.

## Opening paperwork — no account needed

Families **don't need a login** to complete paperwork you send:

1. Kinderly emails them the link and a **6-digit PIN**.
2. They open the link and enter the PIN.
3. Five wrong attempts locks the link for 30 minutes.

That's why you can send paperwork to anyone with an email address — a prospective family, a grandparent, an agency — without setting anything up first.

:::note[Kinderly sends the PIN for you]
You don't have to pass it on. It goes in the same email as the link. The PIN is also shown to you once at creation, as a fallback for when a family says the email never arrived.
:::

The PIN can't be recovered afterwards — Kinderly stores it scrambled. If a family loses it, delete the share and create a new one. See [Shares overview](/shares/overview/#the-pin).

### Links without a PIN

A share can be set to **Public access**, which removes the PIN entirely. Fine for a general enquiry form on your website; not appropriate for anything asking for a child's medical details or a signature. See [Sending a share](/shares/sending/).

## Keeping access tidy

:::caution[Remove access when people leave]
A departing staff member's login should be deactivated the same week. In Manage, set the [staff record](/manage/staff/) to inactive rather than deleting it — deleting takes their attendance history with it, and that history is your time-record evidence.
:::

:::tip[Set expiries on shares]
Every live share is an open door to information about a child. An expiry date closes it for you rather than relying on you remembering. See [Managing shares](/shares/admin/).
:::
