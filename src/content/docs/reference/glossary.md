## The four products

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Enroll** | Forms, documents, packets and shares — the paperwork side of Kinderly. |
| **Manage** | Running your center: children, guardians, staff, classrooms, scheduling, billing, reports. |
| **Grow** | Filling your places: a public website, virtual tours, and an enquiry pipeline. |
| **Funding** | Finding and tracking grants. |

All four are on every plan; tiers differ by limits. See [Plans](/billing/plans/).

## Enroll

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Form** | A reusable set of fields a family fills in. |
| **Document** | A file in your library, usually a PDF, which can carry fillable fields. |
| **Packet** | A multi-step flow combining forms, documents, conditions and actions. |
| **Condition** | A logic node inside a packet that branches the flow on an earlier answer. |
| **Action** | An automated task that runs when a step completes — send an email, create a child record, add a pipeline card. |
| **Share** | A private link delivering a form, document or packet to one family. |
| **PIN** | The 6-digit code protecting a share. Emailed to the recipient with the link, and shown to you once at creation. |
| **Public access** | A share setting that removes the PIN, so anyone with the link can open it. |
| **Approval mode** | A packet setting holding all its actions until someone reviews and approves. |

## Manage

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Classroom** | A room. Drives ratios, alerts and name-to-face checks. |
| **Program** | Something a family enrols in, carrying its fee structure. Drives billing. |
| **Guardian** | Anyone attached to a child — parent, carer, emergency contact. |
| **Primary** | The main contact for a child. Set per child, not per guardian. |
| **Can Pick Up** | Whether a guardian is authorised to collect a child. Separate from being a guardian. |
| **Ratio** | The required staff-to-child ratio for a room, counted from who is *signed in*. |
| **Transition** | A planned move of a child between classrooms. |
| **Billing account** | Who receives the invoice. Can have several holders and cover several children. |
| **Agency ledger** | The portion of a child's fees paid by a third party such as a state subsidy. |
| **CACFP** | The USDA Child and Adult Care Food Program. Tracked per child for claims. |
| **Custom field** | An extra field you define on the child record. |
| **Feed** | Your center's noticeboard — news, announcements, events and closures. |
| **Finance** | Your center's operating spend. Separate from family Billing. |

## Curriculum

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Framework** | A published set of learning standards, e.g. Head Start ELOF or a state's standards. |
| **ELOF** | Head Start Early Learning Outcomes Framework. Used as the common language other frameworks crosswalk to. |
| **Outcome** | A single learning standard within a framework. |
| **Observation** | A note tagged to an outcome, forming your documentation evidence. |
| **Outcome gap** | An outcome with little or no evidence recorded against it. |

## Grow

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Site** | A public website for your center, on a `kinderlysites.com` address. |
| **Page** | One URL on a site. |
| **Section** | A building block stacked to make a page — hero, gallery, FAQ, embedded form. |
| **Draft** | Your unpublished edits. Families see the published version until you publish. |
| **Theme** | Colours, fonts and button styling across the whole site. |
| **Virtual tour** | A photo walkthrough of your space, ending in a call-to-action. |
| **Pipeline** | A board tracking prospective families from enquiry to enrolled. |

## Funding

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Opportunity** | A grant you may be eligible for. |
| **Funding profile** | The answers about your center that decide what you match. |
| **Application** | A tracked opportunity, with its deadline, document checklist and reporting requirements. |
| **Matching funds** | Money or in-kind contribution you must provide to receive a grant. |

## Other

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Ellie** | Kinderly's built-in assistant. Included on every plan. |
| **Team** | Your center. Owns everything and contains your staff logins. |
| **KinderConnect** | The KinderSystems integration for reporting attendance for state subsidy billing. |
